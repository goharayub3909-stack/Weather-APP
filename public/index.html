How risk grades and rolling windows work

The past window contains the last 72 completed model hours. The seven-day outlook shows the highest weather-risk grade across every complete 72-hour window inside the next 168 forecast hours. The seven daily cells each cover a successive 24-hour forecast period from the current model hour. Hour boundaries and dates use Pakistan Standard Time. Missing values never become zero or a low-risk result. Ambiguous research criteria remain disabled for review.

Crop health · field monitoring

Combine nearby station weather with field-level satellite indices. Set your crop and actual stage above, then draw your field below.

No field boundary supplied.

Index legends show their actual ranges and display colours. Water, bare soil, clouds and crop stage affect spectral indices. Field mean, crop health score and yield are not inferred without quality masking and validated field data. Boundaries stay in this browser session.

Indices & limitations

VPD uses temperature and humidity; it is air VPD, not leaf VPD. Delta T is dry-bulb minus supplied wet-bulb temperature, or a labelled Stull approximation. GDD uses capped daily minimum / maximum temperatures and an editable crop base. Crop bases and Kc are starting assumptions; confirm your variety and stage. Simple chilling counts 0–7.2°C hours, not chill portions. ET₀ is reference atmospheric demand; crop ETc depends on your Kc. Soil moisture, actual crop health, accumulated station history and pathogen presence are not supplied by the weather forecast.

PAKISTAN’S GROWING CALENDAR

Seasonal crops

Indicative month ranges, not a planting prescription. Crops shown here are regional possibilities; their presence in your field is not inferred. Set your actual crop and stage above.

How the recommendations work

The calendar is a broad Pakistan season guide. Dates vary by district, elevation, variety, irrigation and planting cycle. The growing-area guess uses coordinates only; change it when it does not fit your field.

Planning triggers: rain ≥20 mm in 24 forecast hours or ≥10 mm in an hour; air temperature ≤2°C for a frost watch (≤0°C for freezing); heat ≥35°C, or ≥30°C for a selected cool-season crop at flowering; gusts ≥35 km/h or sustained wind ≥25 km/h; and six consecutive humid hours (RH ≥85%, 10–30°C) for scouting. These are transparent screening rules, not validated crop-loss thresholds.

Candidate fieldwork periods need at least three daylight hours (06:00–18:00 PKT), temperature 5–35°C, wind ≤20 km/h, gusts ≤30 km/h, and six forecast hours with rain below 0.2 mm/hour and no available model storm/hail signal. They do not establish workable soil or safe pesticide conditions. Follow product labels and on-field measurements for spraying.

Primary inputs: ECMWF IFS via Open-Meteo. Where available, a separate supplemental model supplies labelled thunderstorm/hail signals; CAPE alone never triggers a hail recommendation. Missing values remain unknown and cannot create a dry window. Official warnings and agromet guidance: Pakistan Meteorological Department.
