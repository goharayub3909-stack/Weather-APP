    Trail colour = wind speed (m/s × 3.6 = km/h): white-cyan calm, green, yellow, amber, red, magenta gale. Trails hug the ground; with “Follow the terrain” the 10 m wind is the 1 km mass-consistent field over the GLO-90 terrain when it is loaded (flow goes round peaks and speeds up where the layer thins over ridges, capped at 2×), otherwise a simpler slope rule. A terrain adjustment of the ECMWF wind, not a weather model.
